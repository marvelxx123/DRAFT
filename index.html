FAQ

Common questions

How long does shipping take?

Most orders ship within 48 hours of being placed. Delivery times vary by location: USA 3–5 days, Europe 5–8 days, rest of world 7–14 days. Express options available at checkout.

What file formats can I upload?

PNG, JPG, SVG, and PDF — up to 50MB. For best print results, use PNG with transparent background at least 300 DPI. Our system auto-optimizes your image for print quality.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Zero minimums. Order one piece or one thousand — same price, same quality, same service. Perfect for individuals, brands, and corporate orders alike.

Can I sell these products in my own store?

Yes, absolutely. WEARIT is built for drop-shipping. Connect your Shopify or WooCommerce store, set your own prices, and we handle printing and shipping directly to your customers.

What if the quality isn't right?

We stand behind every order with our 30-day quality guarantee. If anything arrives damaged, misprinted, or wrong — we reprint it at zero cost. No questions asked.

Do you handle custom packaging?

Yes. Premium subscribers can add custom branded packaging — tissue paper, stickers, thank-you cards — shipped directly to your customer with your branding, not ours.
