What We Fix

Every opener problem, solved.

Diagnosis on-site, repair or replacement same day across Jacksonville.

01
Opener Diagnosis

Remote, wall button, and keypad all dead? We test the motor, circuit board, capacitor, sensors, and power supply to find the actual failure — no guessing, no unnecessary parts.

02
Circuit Board Replacement

The most common failure after power surges and lightning (Jacksonville gets plenty of both). We carry circuit boards for LiftMaster and other major brands. Often cheaper than a full replacement.

03
Gear & Sprocket Replacement

Clicking, grinding, or motor running but door not moving? The gear set is usually the culprit. We replace it in the same visit — typically faster than replacing the whole unit.

04
Smart Opener Installation

Upgrade to a Wi-Fi enabled opener — control your garage from your phone, set access schedules, get alerts. myQ-compatible units with full app setup included. Popular in Nocatee and Ponte Vedra new construction.

05
Sensor Repair & Alignment

Door reverses before closing, or blinks 10 times and stops? That's a safety sensor problem — misalignment, dirty lens, or a cut wire. We align and test before leaving.

06
Full Opener Replacement

When repair isn't worth it, we replace the unit — remove the old one, install and align the new unit, program all remotes and keypads. We recommend what makes sense for your door weight and usage.
