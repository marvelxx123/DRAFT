Returns & Refunds

Last Updated: May 2026  ·  Questions? support@wearit.com

Our promise: Every WEARIT product is backed by a 30-day quality guarantee. If something arrives defective, damaged, or wrong, we'll reprint and reship it at no cost to you. No hassle, no questions.

1. Our 30-Day Quality Guarantee

We stand behind every product we print. If your order arrives with any of the following issues, we will reprint and reship it free of charge within the 30-day window from your delivery date:

  • Print defects: Fading, cracking, peeling, smearing, misaligned print, or significant colour inaccuracy compared to your approved preview
  • Manufacturing defects: Stitching failures, holes, tears, or structural damage that occur before use
  • Wrong item: You received a different product, size, colour, or design than what you ordered
  • Significant size discrepancy: The item dimensions are materially outside the manufacturer's stated size chart measurements (more than 1 inch off)
  • Shipping damage: Items visibly damaged during transit, where damage is reported with photographic evidence

To be eligible for a free reprint under our quality guarantee, claims must be submitted within 30 days of the delivery date shown on your tracking information.

2. Custom Products Policy — No Returns on Correct Orders

WEARIT is a print-on-demand service. Every product is manufactured specifically for you after your order is placed — we hold no inventory and cannot resell returned items.

For this reason, we cannot accept returns or issue refunds for custom products that have been correctly printed and delivered as ordered. This is standard practice across the print-on-demand industry and applies in the following situations:

  • You ordered the wrong size — please consult our size guide before ordering
  • You changed your mind after the order was placed
  • The design looks different on the physical product than you imagined (but matches the digital preview you approved)
  • Minor colour variations that fall within the acceptable range of screen-to-print colour differences (monitors and printers use different colour profiles)
  • The product matches your order but you simply don't like it

We strongly encourage you to use our interactive 3D product preview in the design editor before purchasing. If you are unsure about sizing, our size guides are available on each product page.

3. How to Request a Reprint

If your order qualifies for a free reprint under our 30-day quality guarantee, here's how to submit your claim:

  1. Email us at support@wearit.com within 30 days of your delivery date
  2. Use the subject line: Reprint Request — Order #[your order number]
  3. Include in your email:
    • Your order number (found in your confirmation email or account dashboard)
    • A clear description of the issue (e.g., "print is cracked," "received wrong size," "item arrived damaged")
    • At least 2 photographs showing the defect or damage — one close-up and one showing the full item
    • Your preferred replacement (same item, or if the item is discontinued, an alternative)

Our support team will review your claim within 1–2 business days and confirm whether a reprint has been approved. You do not need to return the original defective item unless specifically requested.

4. Refund Timeline

In cases where a refund is applicable (e.g., items that cannot be reprinted due to discontinuation, or where you prefer a refund over a reprint), the following timelines apply:

  • Refund approval: We will notify you of approval within 1–2 business days of receiving your complete claim
  • Refund processing: Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days from the date of approval
  • Bank posting: Once processed by us, refunds are returned to your original payment method. Depending on your card issuer or bank, it may take an additional 3–10 business days for the funds to appear in your account
  • Refunds are issued in US Dollars. If your bank charged a currency conversion fee on your original payment, we cannot refund that fee

We will send you an email confirmation when your refund has been processed. If you do not see the refund within 15 business days, please contact your bank first, then reach out to us at support@wearit.com.

5. Lost in Shipping Policy

We reship orders that are confirmed lost in transit at no additional charge. Here's how we handle lost packages:

What Counts as "Lost in Shipping"

  • Your tracking shows no movement for 15 or more consecutive business days (domestic US orders) or 30 or more business days (international orders)
  • The carrier marks your shipment as "lost" or "undeliverable" in their tracking system
  • Your package never arrived and the tracking information was never updated after the initial scan

What Does Not Qualify

  • Packages marked "Delivered" by the carrier but not received — these require a claim with the carrier. We will assist you with this process and, if the carrier confirms loss, will reship
  • Packages delayed but still in transit (showing active movement)
  • Packages returned to us due to an incorrect address provided by you — we will reship to a corrected address, but additional shipping fees apply

How to Report a Lost Package

  1. Wait until the applicable timeframe has elapsed (15 or 30 business days as above)
  2. Check your tracking link one more time and confirm there has been no recent movement
  3. Email support@wearit.com with subject: Lost Package — Order #[your order number]
  4. Include your order number, the tracking number, and a brief description

We will file a claim with the carrier and, once confirmed, reship your order within 3–5 business days. The reshipment uses the same shipping method as your original order.

6. Cancellations

Because WEARIT products go into production quickly, our cancellation window is very short:

  • Within 1 hour of order placement: Email support@wearit.com immediately. We will attempt to cancel before production begins, but cannot guarantee success.
  • After 1 hour: Orders that have entered production cannot be cancelled. Once your item ships, our standard returns policy applies.

7. Exceptions and Special Cases

  • Sale and promotional items: Items purchased during sales or with discount codes are still covered by our 30-day quality guarantee for defects. The no-return policy for correct custom orders still applies.
  • Gift orders: If you received a WEARIT product as a gift and it arrived defective, the recipient may contact us directly with the order number (available from the gift giver or order confirmation email).
  • Bulk orders (10+ units): Contact us at support@wearit.com before placing bulk orders. Bulk orders may have custom quality assurance and return arrangements.
