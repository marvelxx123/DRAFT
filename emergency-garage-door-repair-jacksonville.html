Emergency Repairs We Handle

Whatever stopped working — we fix it.

Parts on the truck. Most emergencies resolved same day in Jacksonville.

01
Door Stuck Open

A door that won't close is a security emergency. We prioritize these calls — a fully open garage exposes your home and car. We diagnose and fix the cause same day.

02
Car Trapped in Garage

Can't get your car out? Broken spring or dead opener are the most common causes. We carry both on the truck and resolve this in one visit.

03
Broken Spring — Immediate

Spring snap is usually obvious — a loud bang followed by a door that won't budge. Torsion and extension springs in stock for every standard Jacksonville home door.

04
Door Off Track

A door that jumped its track is dangerous to operate and can cause further damage. We realign the door, inspect rollers and cables, and test before leaving.

05
Opener Completely Dead

Neither remote nor wall button works? Could be the circuit board, capacitor, or motor. We diagnose and repair or replace same day — all major brands covered.

06
Snapped Cable

When a lift cable snaps, the door drops unevenly or the bottom bracket can fail. A safety risk that needs immediate attention — we carry cables and hardware on the truck.
