Panel Replacement in Jacksonville

Repair or replace —
we'll tell you honestly.

A garage door panel is one horizontal section of the door. Most residential doors have 4–5 panels stacked vertically. When one panel gets hit by a car, damaged in a storm, or cracks from age, you have two options: replace just that panel, or replace the whole door.

The honest answer depends on the door's age, whether the replacement panel can be sourced as a match, and whether the structural frame is still sound. We'll tell you which makes more financial sense — not just what's a bigger job for us.

If the door is newer (under 10 years), a panel swap often makes sense. If the door is older, full replacement may be more cost-effective long-term. Either way, we give you the full picture before any work starts.

What We Do

Single panel or full door — we do both.

Honest assessment first. We match your door style or help you choose a full replacement.

01
Single Panel Replacement

Replace one damaged panel with a match from the original manufacturer when available. We assess the door's age and whether sourcing a match panel is feasible before committing to this path.

02
Multi-Panel Replacement

Two or more panels damaged? We evaluate whether replacing sections is still cost-effective versus a full door replacement — and give you the numbers for both.

03
Full Door Replacement

Sometimes a full replacement is the smarter investment — especially on older doors. We install new doors from Clopay, Wayne Dalton, and Amarr, properly fitted to your existing tracks.

04
Storm Damage Assessment

After a storm, the visible panel damage may hide frame damage underneath. We inspect the full door system — springs, cables, tracks, and opener — not just the panels you can see.

05
Insurance Documentation

We can provide a written assessment of panel damage for homeowners insurance claims. Photos, damage description, and repair vs. replacement recommendation in writing.

06
Panel Touch-Up & Paint

Minor surface damage or faded paint? Sometimes the cosmetic fix is a touch-up rather than a full replacement. We'll tell you honestly if that's the right call.
