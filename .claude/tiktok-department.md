## What works now (researched Oct 2026)
- TikTok indexes the caption, the on-screen text AND spoken words → say and show the keyword ("garage door repair in Jacksonville").
- 3–5 specific hashtags; never #fyp/#foryou/#viral.
- City/neighborhood in caption + location tag (Local feed launched Feb 2026).
- First 1–2 seconds decide everything: open on the problem, the sound, or the result — never a logo.
- Short (7–30s) for daily posts; repair walk-throughs can run 30–60s.
- Since Apr 2026 creators can review/block/suggest the search keywords TikTok assigns — check them on each post.

## Content pillars (rotate)
1. **Problem → fix** (snapped spring, off-track door, frayed cable): before/after, the "bang" sound.
2. **Is your door OK?** 10-second self-checks homeowners can do (balance test, noisy rollers, sensor alignment) — safety first, never tell people to touch springs/cables.
3. **Day in the truck / same-day call** — speed, parts on every truck, area names (Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, beaches).
4. **Coastal Florida** — salt air rust, hurricane season, humidity on openers.
5. **Satisfying** — smooth quiet door, new opener install, lubrication close-ups.
6. **Proof** — real Thumbtack review read out loud (27 reviews, 5.0).
