Why Garage Doors Fail Faster on Amelia Island

Fernandina Beach and Amelia Island sit directly on the Atlantic coast. That means salt air, constant humidity, and tropical storm exposure year-round. Here's what that does to garage doors — and what we do about it.

Salt Air Corrosion

Ocean salt is microscopic — it travels in the air and settles on every metal surface. Bare steel torsion springs can start corroding from the inside out within months of installation on Amelia Island. The failure happens suddenly, usually with a loud bang when the spring snaps. We install only galvanized springs on coastal properties.

Hurricane & Tropical Storm Damage

Fernandina Beach is in Nassau County's coastal wind-borne debris region. Standard garage doors are often the most vulnerable point of a home during a storm — they can fail inward under sustained wind pressure, allowing interior damage. Wind-rated doors are a meaningful upgrade here.

Heat & Humidity Expansion

Florida's summer heat causes door panels and frames to expand, while high humidity promotes rust on any exposed hardware. Doors that operate perfectly in February may start binding or scraping by July. Seasonal tune-ups — especially in spring before hurricane season — catch these issues early.

Older Home Door Sizes

Fernandina Beach's historic district has homes with non-standard door openings that don't take off-the-shelf door sizes. We measure before ordering and have experience sourcing doors for older construction on Amelia Island — something a drive-by national chain won't bother with.
