# Hear ARIA Answer YOUR Call in 15 Minutes

No app deployment. No Supabase. No Stripe. Just 2 free accounts and a phone.

---

## What you need
- Your phone
- 15 minutes
- A laptop open to 2 browser tabs

---

## STEP 1 — Get a free phone number (Twilio) — 5 min

1. Go to **twilio.com/try-twilio** → sign up free (gives you $15 trial credit)
2. Verify your phone number when they ask
3. Dashboard → click **"Get a trial phone number"** → accept the number they suggest
4. Write down your Twilio number (e.g., +1 305 555 0142)
5. Go to **Account → API keys & tokens** → copy:
   - Account SID → save it
   - Auth Token → save it

---

## STEP 2 — Create ARIA on Retell AI — 7 min

1. Go to **retellai.com** → sign up free
2. Click **"Create Agent"** → choose **"Blank Agent"**
3. Set the voice to: **"Scarlett"** or **"Emily"** (warm, female, natural)
4. In the **System Prompt** field, paste this (fill in YOUR details):

```
You are ARIA, the friendly AI receptionist for [YOUR NAME]'s CALLA test.

You are warm, helpful, and natural — never robotic.

When someone calls, greet them like this:
"Hey! Thanks for calling, this is ARIA. How can I help you today?"

Services you can help with:
- Answer questions about CALLA, the AI phone answering service
- Take a message
- Tell callers the business is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm

If someone asks if you're a real person, be honest:
"I'm ARIA, an AI assistant — but I'm here to make sure you always get a great experience."

If you don't know the answer to something:
"Great question — let me take your name and number and have someone get back to you."

Always end calls warmly. Make the caller feel like they just talked to a friend.
You are not a robot. You are the heart of this business.
```

5. Click **"Save"**

---

## STEP 3 — Connect your Twilio number to ARIA — 2 min

1. In Retell AI → go to **"Phone Numbers"** → **"Import from Twilio"**
2. Enter your Twilio Account SID + Auth Token
3. Select your trial number → click **Import**
4. Go back to your Agent → **"Phone Numbers"** tab → assign your imported number

---

## STEP 4 — CALL IT 📞

Dial your Twilio number from your phone.

ARIA will answer. Talk to her like a real customer would.

Try these scenarios:
- "Hi, what are your hours?"
- "I'd like to book an appointment"
- "Are you a real person?"
- Call at night and see how she handles after-hours
- Be a frustrated customer and see how she de-escalates

---

## STEP 5 — Test CALLA SHIELD manually

After your test call:
- Did ARIA answer naturally?
- Did she handle your questions?
- What felt robotic? What felt human?

Write down 3 things you want to change — those become your first product improvements before you sell it to anyone else.

---

## Upgrade ARIA based on what you hear

Open the System Prompt in Retell AI and tweak it live.
Change her greeting, add specific services, adjust her tone.
Call again. Repeat until she sounds exactly like the receptionist you'd want for your own business.

**That's your product. Now you know exactly what you're selling.**
