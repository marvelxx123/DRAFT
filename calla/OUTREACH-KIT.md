# CALLA — Complete Outreach & Sales Kit

> For internal use. These scripts are written to convert, not impress. Use them verbatim, then adapt based on what works.

---

## 1. COLD DM SCRIPTS (Instagram / Facebook)

Keep it under 5 lines. No links in the first message. No "I hope this finds you well." Just a human talking to a human.

---

### Variation A — Missed Calls Angle

> Hey [Name]! Love what you're doing at [Salon Name] — your color work is stunning. Quick question: do you ever miss calls while you're mid-highlight? We built something specifically for that. Mind if I share how it works?

---

### Variation B — After-Hours Angle

> Hey [Name]! I came across [Salon Name] and love your vibe. Random but — what happens when someone calls you at 9pm on a Sunday to book? We help salons capture those bookings automatically. Want me to show you how?

---

### Variation C — Google Reviews Angle

> Hey [Name]! Your work at [Salon Name] is gorgeous — seriously. I noticed you have [X] reviews on Google. We have a free tool that gets salons like yours a steady stream of new 5-star reviews on autopilot. Worth a 5-minute look?

---

**DM Tips:**
- Personalize the opening with something real (a post, a photo, their review count)
- Don't pitch in the first message — ask a question
- If they respond, move to a voice note or offer a 10-min call, not a wall of text

## 4. DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT (10–15 Minutes)

This is a conversation, not a presentation. Your job is to listen more than you talk for the first 5 minutes.

---

### Opening (0:00–1:00)

> "Thanks for making time — I'll keep this tight, I know you're running a salon. Before I tell you anything about CALLA, I just want to understand how things work for you right now. Is that okay?"

**Why this works:** It disarms them. Most salespeople jump into demos. You're different.

---

### Pain Discovery Questions (1:00–5:00)

Ask these in order. Let them talk. Don't interrupt.

1. **"How do clients typically book with you right now — phone, Instagram DMs, a booking app?"**
   *(Listen for: chaos, multiple channels, inconsistency)*

2. **"What happens when the phone rings while you're with a client? Who picks it up?"**
   *(Listen for: "it just rings," "I call back later," "my receptionist... sometimes")*

3. **"Have you ever estimated how many calls you miss in a week?"**
   *(Listen for: "I try not to think about it" — that's your opening)*

4. **"And when someone calls after hours — like a Sunday night — what happens to that call?"**
   *(Listen for: voicemail, nothing, lost)*

5. **"How are you doing on Google reviews? Are clients leaving them consistently or is it kind of random?"**
   *(Listen for: "I wish there was an easy way" or "I always forget to ask")*

---

### Transition to Demo (5:00–7:00)

> "Okay, this is making total sense. Everything you just described is exactly what CALLA was built for. Can I show you something real quick — I'm going to call your salon number right now from my phone, and you can hear what your clients would hear."

**[Call their salon number from your phone. CALLA will answer. Let them hear it.]*

> "That's what your clients hear at 11pm on a Saturday when they want to book. CALLA books them, captures their info, and sends you a summary. You wake up to a booked appointment."

---

### CALLA SHIELD Demo (7:00–9:00)

> "The part most owners get excited about is something we call CALLA SHIELD. After every call, CALLA sends the client an automatic text — like 'Thanks for calling [Salon Name]! How was your experience?' If they're happy, it walks them right to your Google page to leave a review. You don't do anything. It just... happens."

> "You mentioned you have [X] reviews right now. Most of our salons see that number start climbing in the first two weeks without asking a single client manually. That's the part that usually gets people."

---

### Qualifying Close (9:00–11:00)

> "So here's where we are — based on what you told me, you're losing bookings on missed calls, you don't have a consistent review strategy, and this could run on autopilot for you. Does that track?"

**[Let them confirm]**

> "Cool. I want to make this as low-risk as possible. We have a 14-day free trial — no card needed until after you've actually seen it work. I can have your CALLA live in about 15 minutes. Want to do that today?"

---

### If They Don't Say Yes Immediately

> "Totally fair. What would you need to see to feel confident about this?"

Then shut up and listen.

---

### Close: Trial Offer Script

> "Here's what I'd suggest: let's get you set up on the free trial right now. Takes about 15 minutes — I'll ask you a few quick questions about your salon, we'll configure your CALLA AI together, and you'll have it live by end of day. You try it for two weeks. If you book one client you would've missed, it's already paid for itself. Sound fair?"

## 5. OBJECTION HANDLING PLAYBOOK

Don't argue. Agree, pivot, and redirect.

---

### "We already have voicemail."

> "Totally — voicemail catches the call. But here's the thing: most people don't leave voicemails anymore. They just hang up and book somewhere else. CALLA actually *talks* to them, answers their questions, and locks in the appointment right then. Voicemail can't do that at 10pm."

---

### "We tried AI before and it was bad."

> "I hear that a lot, honestly. Most AI phone tools sound robotic and frustrate people. CALLA is different — it's built specifically for salons, so it knows how to handle appointment booking, service questions, pricing. I'd love to call your number right now and just let you hear it. If it sounds bad, you'll know immediately and we'll stop there."

*(Always offer the live demo when this objection comes up.)*

---

### "It's too expensive."

> "I get it — $79/month feels like one more thing. But let me flip it around: how many calls do you miss in a week? Even two. At $100 average per appointment, that's $800 a month walking out the door. CALLA pays for itself on the first booking it captures. If it doesn't, cancel — there's no contract."

---

### "I need to think about it."

> "Of course — what's the main thing you want to think through? Is it the price, the tech, whether your clients would like it...?"

*(Wait for them to specify. The real objection is hiding inside "I need to think about it." Find it.)*

If they stay vague:

> "Here's what I'd suggest — let's get the free trial running so you're thinking about it with real data instead of hypotheticals. Two weeks, no card. If you think about it and it's a no, just let me know. But at least you'll know for sure."

---

### "My clients prefer talking to a real person."

> "That's a great instinct — and honestly, your clients calling to book an appointment aren't looking for a deep conversation. They want to know if you're free Thursday at 2pm. CALLA handles that perfectly, and the clients who want to talk to you personally can always be transferred or called back. You stay in the relationship; CALLA handles the logistics."

## 6. ONBOARDING SCRIPT

This is what you do the day they sign up. First impressions set the tone for retention.

---

### Welcome Message (Send immediately after signup)

> "Hey [Name]! Welcome to CALLA — so glad to have [Salon Name] on board. You made a great call (pun intended).
>
> Here's what happens next: I'm going to send you 5 quick questions. Once you answer them, your CALLA AI will be live and answering phones for you — usually within a few hours.
>
> This is going to feel weirdly easy. Let's get you set up."

---

### The 5 Configuration Questions (ask these on a setup call or via form)

Walk through these with them — takes 10–15 minutes max.

**Question 1: What's your salon's name and how do you want CALLA to greet callers?**
> "For example: 'Thank you for calling Luxe Salon, I'm CALLA — how can I help you today?' Sound right or want to tweak it?"

**Question 2: What services do you offer, and what are your general prices?**
> "CALLA will handle 'how much does a balayage cost' questions automatically once we load this in. Just give me a rough list — doesn't need to be exact."

**Question 3: What are your hours, and do you want CALLA to book appointments directly or take a message for you to confirm?**
> "Most salons start with 'take a message + confirm' until they trust the system, then switch to direct booking. What feels right to start?"

**Question 4: What's the one thing you never want CALLA to say or do on a call?**
> "For example: some owners don't want pricing mentioned, some don't want cancellation policies discussed. Anything like that?"

**Question 5: What's the phone number or text line where you want CALLA to send you summaries after each call?**
> "You'll get a quick text after every call — who answered, what they wanted, and whether they booked. What number do you want that going to?"

---

### Post-Setup Confirmation — What to Send Them

Send this within an hour of going live:

> "You're live! Here's a quick summary of what's set up:
>
> - **CALLA is answering:** [Their phone number]
> - **Booking mode:** [Direct / Message + Confirm]
> - **CALLA SHIELD:** Active — clients will get a follow-up text after every call and be guided to leave a Google review if they're happy
> - **Your summary texts go to:** [Their number]
>
> **Quick test:** Call your own number right now and hear what your clients hear. If anything sounds off, reply here and we'll fix it in minutes.
>
> You're all set. Go back to focusing on your clients — CALLA has the phones."

---

**Onboarding Notes:**
- Call or text them on Day 3 to check in: "How's CALLA doing? Any calls you want to review?"
- On Day 7, pull their call summary and send them a highlight: "You captured X calls this week — here's one that would have been a missed booking."
- On Day 14, remind them the trial is ending and make the conversion easy: send a payment link with a one-click signup, no forms to fill out
