## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

CALLA is a white-label AI phone answering service resold to small businesses under a subscription model. The core differentiator is **CALLA SHIELD** — a free post-call layer that sends an SMS recap to the business owner, detects caller sentiment, and (for positive calls) automatically prompts the caller to leave a Google review. No competitor in the SMB AI answering space includes this for free. Launch niche: **hair salons and beauty studios** (explained in Section 2). Path to $10k MRR in 90 days, $50k MRR by Month 10.

### 1.3 Unit Economics

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
- Month 1–3 target: $80–$120 (organic + direct outreach, near-zero ad spend)
- Month 4–12 target: $150–$200 (mix of paid + organic as you scale)
- Use this benchmark: if paid ads cost $5/click with a 3% trial-to-paid conversion and $149 ACV, your CAC from ads is $167. Cold email/DM at scale can get this under $50.

**Lifetime Value (LTV)**
- Target monthly churn: 3% (industry SMB SaaS avg is 5–8% — CALLA SHIELD creates retention leverage, aiming lower)
- Average customer lifespan at 3% monthly churn: ~33 months
- Average ACV (blended tier): $149/month
- Upsell attach rate target: 40% of customers add at least one $29–$49 add-on
- Blended ARPU with upsells: ~$175/month
- **LTV = $175 × 33 = $5,775**

**LTV:CAC Target**
- Month 1–3: LTV:CAC = $5,775 / $100 = **57:1** (outreach-heavy, near-zero CAC)
- Month 6–12: LTV:CAC = $5,775 / $175 = **33:1** (still excellent; industry healthy = 3:1)

**Payback Period**
- At $149/month with $87% gross margin ($130 gross profit/month) and $150 CAC: payback = **1.2 months**
- This is exceptional. Means the business can reinvest revenue into growth almost immediately.

## 2. GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY — FIRST 90 DAYS

### 2.1 Primary Niche: HAIR SALONS AND BEAUTY STUDIOS

**Why salons, not the other four options:**

1. **Volume of missed calls is the core pain.** A busy salon misses 30–60% of incoming calls during peak hours. Each missed call = $50–$200 lost booking. This pain is visceral and immediate — owners feel it daily.

2. **Low technical resistance.** Salon owners are not sophisticated software buyers. They do not want to learn a platform. They want it to work. CALLA's 15-minute setup is a direct fit.

3. **Google reviews are existential.** Salons live and die by Google Maps ratings. CALLA SHIELD's automatic Google review prompt after a good call is not a feature — it is survival. This resonates instantly with any salon owner.

4. **High referral density.** Salons talk to each other at trade shows, supplier events, and on Instagram. One happy salon in a city will tell three others. The word-of-mouth coefficient is high.

5. **Existing software fatigue opens the door.** Many salons use Vagaro, Fresha, or Square. These tools do NOT handle live call answering. CALLA is not a competitor — it is a complement. Partnership angle exists immediately.

6. **Fast sales cycle.** A salon owner can make a $149/month decision in one conversation without a procurement committee, legal review, or IT approval.

**Secondary niches for Months 3–6 (after playbook is proven):** HVAC companies (high ticket per call, seasonal volume spikes, owner-operated), real estate agents (always on the go, hate missing buyer leads).

### 2.3 How to Get the First 10 Paying Clients

**Week 1–2: Zero-budget founder hustle**

1. Walk into 5 local salons in person. Ask to speak to the owner. Do NOT pitch. Ask: "How do you handle calls when everyone's busy?" Listen for 3 minutes. Then say: "Can I show you something on my phone that might fix that?" — live demo on the spot.

2. Post in 3 local Facebook groups for small business owners: "I'm looking for 5 salons to try a free 2-week trial of an AI answering service. In exchange for honest feedback. DM me." Cap responses at 10.

3. LinkedIn: Search "salon owner [your city]". Connect with 20 per day. Use the DM script above. No automation yet — manual sends only so you can personalize.

4. Find the top 20 salons in your city with under 4.2 Google stars or under 50 reviews. These owners feel the Google problem acutely. Cold email them Template A with subject "Your salon's Google reviews."

5. Join one local BNI chapter or Chamber of Commerce networking event. Attend once. Your 60-second pitch: "I help hair salons stop losing bookings to voicemail and get more Google reviews automatically — without doing anything extra." Get 3 business cards minimum.

**Weeks 3–4: Convert trial users to paid**

- Offer the first 10 clients a **founding member rate**: lock in $99/month for life (instead of $149) if they pay monthly and refer one other business within 60 days. This creates urgency, locks in loyalty, and seeds your referral engine.
- Do a 15-minute check-in call at Day 7 with every trial user. Ask: "What's one thing that would make this perfect?" Capture feedback. Fix what you can. This call alone dramatically increases trial-to-paid conversion.

**Target: 10 paid clients by Day 45. At $99–$149 average = $990–$1,490 MRR by end of Month 2.**

## 3. SALES PROCESS

### 3.1 Discovery Call Script Outline

**Call structure: 25 minutes total**

**Minutes 0–3: Establish rapport and agenda**
> "Thanks for making time. I want to make sure this is worth 25 minutes for you — my goal is to understand how your business handles calls today, show you exactly how CALLA works in about 10 minutes, and then you tell me if it's something worth trying. Sound good?"

**Minutes 3–10: Discovery questions (listen 80%, talk 20%)**
- "Walk me through what happens when your phone rings and everyone's busy with a client."
- "How many calls do you think you miss in a typical week? What do you think each of those is worth?"
- "When someone books with you — do they usually call, text, or go online?"
- "How's your Google review count? Are you happy with where it is compared to competitors?"
- "Have you ever tried any kind of answering service or AI tool before?"

**Capture their answers mentally. Your demo should address their specific answers, not a generic script.**

**Minutes 10–20: Live demo (screen share or phone demo)**
- Show an actual call being handled by the AI in real time (have a pre-recorded demo call ready OR call your own CALLA demo line live)
- Show the post-call SMS recap that goes to the owner's phone
- Show the Google review prompt that goes to the caller
- Say: "This is what your clients would experience. This is what you'd see after every call."

**Minutes 20–25: Close and next step**
> "Based on what you told me — you're missing [X calls/week] and you want more Google reviews — does this seem like something that would actually help?"

If yes: "Great. I can have you live in about 15 minutes right now, or we can schedule it for tomorrow. Which works better?"

If hesitant: "What would need to be true for you to feel confident trying this?" — then handle the objection (see 3.2).

**Never end a call without a next step. Either they're signing up, or you have a specific follow-up scheduled.**

### 3.2 Objection Handling

**Objection 1: "It's too expensive."**

> "I hear that. Can I ask — what do you think one missed booking is worth for you? [Let them answer — usually $100–$200.] CALLA costs $149/month. If it catches even two calls a month that would've gone to voicemail, it's already paid for itself. And that's before the Google reviews, which most of my clients say is the part they can't believe is free. Does that math feel different?"

If still resistant: "What if we started with a 14-day trial so you can see the actual calls it handles before you commit? You can cancel before you're ever charged."

---

**Objection 2: "We already have voicemail."**

> "Totally makes sense — voicemail is fine for messages. The issue is most callers don't leave them. Studies show 80% of callers hang up when they hit voicemail and call the next business on the list. CALLA actually talks to them, answers their question, books the appointment, and sends them a Google review link — all before they can hang up. Voicemail can't do any of that. Want me to show you what the caller experience sounds like?"

---

**Objection 3: "We tried AI before and it didn't work."**

> "That's really helpful to know — what did you try, and what specifically didn't work?"

[Let them explain. Common answers: "It sounded robotic," "It couldn't handle our questions," "Setup was a nightmare."]

> "Okay, so [reflect their specific issue]. That's exactly what CALLA was designed around. The voice is natural — most callers don't know it's AI. And I can have you live in 15 minutes, so you're not committing to a multi-week setup. Why don't I just call your number right now and show you what your clients would actually hear? Then you can judge for yourself."

**The live demo on their own number dissolves this objection faster than any words can.**

---

**Objection 4: "I need to think about it / talk to my partner."**

> "Of course. What's the main thing you're uncertain about? [Listen.] And when you talk to your partner, what would need to be answered for them to be comfortable saying yes?"

Offer to send a one-page summary they can share. Offer a joint call with the partner. Set a specific follow-up time: "Should we reconnect Thursday at the same time?" Do not leave it open-ended.

### 3.3 Onboarding Flow — Client Signed to Live in Under 15 Minutes

**Step 1 (Minutes 0–3): Collect basics via onboarding form**
- Business name, address, website
- Owner's cell (for SHIELD SMS recap)
- Google Business Profile link
- Primary call types (booking, general questions, complaints, directions)

**Step 2 (Minutes 3–7): Configure the AI**
- Log into white-label platform backend
- Create their agent with business name, hours, services, FAQs
- Upload or paste their service menu and pricing (even approximate is fine — can update later)
- Set the SHIELD SMS destination (owner's cell)
- Input their Google review link (shortened URL)

**Step 3 (Minutes 7–10): Provision phone number**
- Assign a local area code number via Twilio (automated in the backend — takes 30 seconds)
- This number is CALLA's number for their business
- Test by calling it once — confirm AI answers correctly

**Step 4 (Minutes 10–13): Set up call forwarding on their existing number**
- Walk the client through forwarding their existing business line to the new CALLA number
- This takes 3 minutes on any phone system: AT&T, Verizon, Google Voice, Vonage, Ring Central
- Have a one-page cheat sheet PDF for each carrier to paste or screen share

**Step 5 (Minutes 13–15): Live test call**
- Call their original business number together while on the demo call
- They hear CALLA answer in real time
- SHIELD SMS arrives on their phone within 30 seconds of hang-up

**Deliverables sent within 1 hour of onboarding:**
- Welcome email with login credentials to their CALLA dashboard
- PDF quick-start guide
- Your direct cell number for any first-week questions
- 7-day check-in call booked

**This 15-minute onboarding is itself a competitive moat — most competitors take days or weeks to set up.**

## 6. COMPETITIVE MOAT

### 6.1 Why CALLA SHIELD Creates Lock-In

CALLA SHIELD is not just a feature — it is a business outcome engine. Here is the lock-in mechanism:

**The flywheel:**
1. CALLA answers a call from a happy client
2. SHIELD detects positive sentiment in the conversation (tone analysis, key phrases like "great," "love it," "can't wait")
3. Within 30 seconds of the call ending, the caller receives an SMS: "Thanks for calling [Salon Name]! Would you take 30 seconds to share your experience? It helps us a lot. [Google Review Link]"
4. Conversion rate on this prompt is estimated at 15–25% (vs. 2–5% for in-person asks that feel awkward)
5. Salon accumulates 8–15 new reviews per month passively
6. Salon's Google Maps ranking improves — more calls come in — CALLA handles them — more reviews

**The lock-in:** After 6 months, a client using CALLA has 50–90 more Google reviews than they would without it. Their Google Maps ranking has visibly improved. Their star rating has stabilized or risen. The moment they cancel CALLA:
- New reviews stop accruing automatically
- They lose their AI answering backup
- They lose the call-by-call SMS recap their owner has come to rely on

The perceived cost of cancellation is not "$149/month saved" — it is "losing my Google review engine, my call backup, and my daily call summary." That is an entirely different psychological calculation.

**Competitor replication barrier:** Competitors can eventually copy CALLA SHIELD. But building it requires:
- Real-time sentiment analysis pipeline on call audio
- Twilio/SMS integration with triggering logic
- Google Business Profile API integration or review link management
- Timing logic that works across call types (don't prompt angry callers)

This is a 3–6 month engineering sprint for a competitor. During that window, CALLA can accumulate 200+ clients whose switching cost has been elevated. By the time a competitor ships a copycat, CALLA's referral network and brand association with this feature is established.

### 6.2 How to Make Switching Painful

**Data gravity:** Every call summary, every sentiment report, every monthly review count dashboard lives inside CALLA. After 6 months, a client has a rich history of call data that disappears if they leave. Consider adding:
- Monthly "CALLA Score" — a composite score of calls handled, reviews generated, missed calls prevented — sent to the owner via email. This makes CALLA's value concrete and visible.
- Annual "Year in Review" report: "CALLA answered 1,847 calls for [Salon Name] this year. We generated 94 new Google reviews. Here's how your busiest days looked." This report alone can prevent cancellation during the dangerous Month 3–6 "is this worth it?" window.

**Workflow integration:** As clients adopt CALLA BOOK and CALLA CRM Sync, CALLA becomes embedded in their daily operating stack. The more they integrate, the higher the switching cost. Every integration is a retention mechanism.

**Phone number ownership:** By default, CALLA provisions the answering number. If the client also chose to port their primary business number INTO CALLA, porting it back out is a 2–4 week process. This is a natural friction barrier, though it should never be used as a hostile retention tactic — just be aware of it as a passive switching cost.

**Community:** Build a CALLA private Facebook group or Slack for clients. "CALLA Pros Network" — salon owners sharing tips, marketing strategies, how to respond to negative reviews. Community creates social switching cost: "If I leave CALLA, I lose access to this group." This is zero cost to build and meaningfully increases retention.
