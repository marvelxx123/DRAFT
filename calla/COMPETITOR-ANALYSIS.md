### 1. MyAIFrontDesk (myaifrontdesk.com)

**Core Features**
- 24/7 AI voice receptionist with neural TTS (natural-sounding voice)
- 20+ language support including multilingual call handling
- Voice cloning (sound like the business owner)
- Built-in chatbot + SMS + email draft automation
- Knowledge base with sequence automation workflows
- Zapier integration; verified outbound caller ID
- Warm call transfers to human staff
- CRM-style contact logging

**Pricing**
| Plan | Cost | Included |
|------|------|----------|
| Free | $0/mo | 20 voice min, 10 chatbot convos, 40 SMS |
| Business-in-a-Box | $99/mo ($79/mo annual) | 200 voice min, 100 chatbot, 400 SMS, 1,000 overage credits/mo |
| Partner/Enterprise | Custom | API access, white-label, dedicated manager |

**Gaps & Pain Points**
- Per-minute billing — cost unpredictable; small plans hit ceilings fast
- Business plan caps concurrent calls at 2 — kills busier operations
- No native CRM sync beyond Zapier (no direct HubSpot, Salesforce, Jobber)
- Monthly credit expiration — unused minutes don't roll over
- No HIPAA/SOC2 compliance on standard plans

## PART 3: TOP 3 PAIN POINTS NO COMPETITOR SOLVES

### #1 — The Amnesia Problem (Caller Memory)
Every competitor treats every call as a fresh start. No persistent caller identity, no history surfaced in real-time. The AI can never say "Welcome back, Mr. Torres — last time you asked about the water heater quote, ready to schedule?" CRMs are record-keeping layers, not live context engines. **This is the #1 frustration in AI interactions: having to repeat yourself.**

### #2 — Nobody Closes the Loop Proactively
All competitors are reactive — they answer calls, some send a follow-up SMS, and then stop. Not one tracks caller intent over time and initiates outreach autonomously ("caller inquired twice, no booking — trigger warm call Thursday 2pm"). **The phone should call the customer, not just wait for them to call back.**

### #3 — No HIPAA-Ready AI at SMB Pricing
Healthcare, legal, and financial services = enormous SMB call volume. Every competitor either ignores compliance entirely or locks it behind $500+/mo enterprise plans. **A dental office, solo therapist, or personal injury attorney has NO viable compliant option at $99–$199/mo. First mover here owns the medical and legal verticals — highest LTV, lowest churn.**

---

## PART 4: MOST VULNERABLE COMPETITOR — ROSIE AI

**Rosie is feature-thin, acquisition-dependent, and actively punishing its own customers.**

1. Key features locked behind $149/mo — entry plans are bait-and-switch
2. Appointment scheduling is a lie: sends a link, doesn't know live calendar
3. English-only on entry plans — excludes Spanish-speaking market
4. No analytics, no CRM, no outbound
5. Documented billing abuse: surprise overages, refused refunds
6. Built for solo practitioners — any growing business churns out

**CALLA should target Rosie customers first.** Price-sensitive, non-technical, actively underserved. They need very little to switch.

**Runner-up: SkipCalls.** Flat unlimited is economically unsustainable. No enterprise path. It has a ceiling, not a moat.
