# How to Forward Your Number to CALLA

You only need to do this once. It takes about 30 seconds.

Your CALLA number is the number we gave you when you signed up. Forward your main business number to that number, and CALLA picks up every call from that point on.

---

## iPhone

1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Phone**
3. Tap **Call Forwarding**
4. Flip the switch to **ON**
5. Tap **Forward To**
6. Type in your CALLA number and tap **Back**

That's it. You'll see a small phone icon with an arrow appear in your status bar at the top of the screen. That means it's working.

**Test it:** Have someone call your main business number. Your CALLA number should ring (or ARIA should answer directly).

---

## Android — Samsung

1. Open the **Phone** app
2. Tap the **three dots** (top right corner)
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Tap **Supplementary services** (or **More settings** on some models)
5. Tap **Call forwarding**
6. Tap **Always forward**
7. Type in your CALLA number
8. Tap **Enable**

**Test it:** Call your business number from another phone. ARIA should answer.

---

## Android — Google Pixel

1. Open the **Phone** app
2. Tap the **three dots** (top right corner)
3. Tap **Settings**
4. Tap **Calls**
5. Tap **Call forwarding**
6. Tap **Always forward**
7. Type in your CALLA number
8. Tap **Turn on**

**Test it:** Call your business number from another phone. ARIA should answer.

## Landline or VoIP — RingCentral

1. Log in at **app.ringcentral.com**
2. Click your profile icon (top right)
3. Click **Settings**
4. Click **Call Handling & Forwarding**
5. Under "If no one answers," select **Forward to** and enter your CALLA number
6. To forward ALL calls: click **Routing** and add your CALLA number as the first destination, set to ring immediately

**Test it:** Call your RingCentral number. ARIA should answer.

---

## Landline or VoIP — Google Voice

1. Go to **voice.google.com**
2. Click the gear icon (top right) → **Settings**
3. Click **Calls**
4. Under "Call forwarding," click **Add a forwarding phone**
5. Enter your CALLA number and verify it
6. Make sure that number is checked/active

**Test it:** Call your Google Voice number. ARIA should answer.

---

## Landline or VoIP — Grasshopper

1. Log in at **app.grasshopper.com**
2. Click **Settings** → **Extensions**
3. Click the extension you want to forward
4. Under **Call Forwarding**, click **Add Number**
5. Type in your CALLA number
6. Set it to ring first (0 second delay) if you want ARIA to always answer, or set a delay if you want the option to grab the call yourself first

**Test it:** Call your Grasshopper number. ARIA should answer.

---

## Landline or VoIP — Nextiva

1. Log in at **nextiva.com/login**
2. Go to **My Account** → **Features** → **Call Forwarding**
3. Select **Always** or **When Unanswered**
4. Enter your CALLA number
5. Click **Save**

**Test it:** Call your Nextiva number. ARIA should answer.

## Carrier-Level Forwarding (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile)

This is the fastest method. You just dial a short code from your phone. No apps, no menus.

### Forward ALL calls

| Carrier | What to dial |
|---|---|
| AT&T | `*72` then your CALLA number, then press call |
| Verizon | `*72` then your CALLA number, then press call |
| T-Mobile | `**21*` then your CALLA number then `#`, press call |

**Example (AT&T/Verizon):** Dial `*72 305-555-0100` and press the call button. Wait for a confirmation tone or message. Hang up. Done.

**Example (T-Mobile):** Dial `**21*+13055550100#` and press call.

### To turn it off

| Carrier | What to dial |
|---|---|
| AT&T | `*73` then press call |
| Verizon | `*73` then press call |
| T-Mobile | `##21#` then press call |

**Test it:** Call your business number from another phone. ARIA should answer.

## What if I Only Want to Forward Missed Calls?

This is called **conditional forwarding** — your phone rings first, and if you don't pick up, CALLA takes over. Here's how:

### iPhone — Conditional (missed calls only)

Apple doesn't give you a built-in "forward on no answer" toggle in Settings. Use the carrier code instead:

Dial: `*61*` then your CALLA number then `*11*20#` and press call.

Example: `*61*13055550100*11*20#`

The `20` means it rings for 20 seconds before forwarding. Change it to `30` if you want more time.

To cancel: dial `##61#` and press call.

### Android — Conditional (missed calls only)

1. Open **Phone** app → Settings → Supplementary Services (Samsung) or Calls (Pixel)
2. Tap **Call forwarding**
3. Tap **When unanswered** (instead of "Always forward")
4. Enter your CALLA number
5. Set the ring time (15–20 seconds is a good balance)
6. Tap **Enable**

Now your phone rings first. If you pick up, it's yours. If you don't, ARIA gets it.

## What About After-Hours Only?

You don't need to mess with your phone settings for this. CALLA handles it automatically.

When you set your business hours inside CALLA, ARIA already knows your schedule. During business hours she answers like a full receptionist. After hours she answers with a different tone — "Hey, we're closed right now, but I'm absolutely here to help."

There's nothing extra to set up on your phone. Just make sure your business hours are correct in your CALLA dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem 1: The call goes to my own voicemail instead of CALLA.**

Your phone's built-in voicemail is intercepting the call before forwarding kicks in. Fix: turn off your carrier voicemail (call your carrier and ask them to disable it), or shorten your voicemail ring time to 5 seconds so forwarding fires first.

Carrier voicemail disable codes:
- AT&T: dial `#004#` and press call
- Verizon: call 1-800-922-0204 and ask to disable voicemail
- T-Mobile: dial `##004#` and press call

**Problem 2: ARIA answers but the audio cuts in and out.**

This is almost always a Wi-Fi/data issue on the caller's end, not CALLA. Ask the caller to try again. If it keeps happening, let us know and we'll check the call logs.

**Problem 3: I dialed the short code but nothing happened.**

Make sure you're not on Wi-Fi calling. Turn off Wi-Fi before dialing the short code, then turn it back on after. Some carriers don't process short codes over Wi-Fi calling.

If you're on a landline: short codes may not work. Use your VoIP provider's web portal instead (see RingCentral, Google Voice, etc. sections above).
