Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026  ·  Effective: May 1, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains exactly what data WEARIT collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your information. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Who We Are

WEARIT ("we," "us," or "our") is a custom print-on-demand platform operating at wearit.com. We connect customers with print fulfillment services to deliver personalized apparel and accessories. For GDPR purposes, WEARIT is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or our data practices, contact us at privacy@wearit.com.

2. Data We Collect

Account & Identity Data

When you create an account or place an order, we collect:

  • Full name — used to personalise your account and address shipping labels
  • Email address — used for order confirmations, shipping updates, and (with consent) marketing
  • Password — stored as a salted cryptographic hash; we never store plaintext passwords

Order & Shipping Data

  • Shipping address (street, city, state/province, postcode, country) — required to fulfil and deliver your order
  • Order history — products ordered, quantities, customisations, and order status
  • Phone number (optional) — provided to shipping carriers for delivery notifications only

Payment Data

All payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not store your card number, CVV, or full payment details on our servers. We retain only:

  • A Stripe customer token (an anonymised reference ID)
  • The last four digits of your card and card brand (for display in your account)
  • Billing address (for fraud prevention)

Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

Design Uploads & AI-Generated Content

When you use the WEARIT design editor, we collect:

  • Uploaded images and artwork — stored securely to render your product preview and process your order
  • AI design prompts — text prompts you submit to our AI design assistant are processed by Anthropic's Claude API. Prompts and outputs may be logged for abuse prevention. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Design configurations — colours, text, placement, product selections saved to your account

Technical & Usage Data

We automatically collect certain technical data when you use our website:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/region level)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time on page, click interactions, and referral source
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Session duration and navigation patterns

This data is collected via Google Analytics and our server access logs. It does not directly identify you.

Communications Data

  • Emails or messages you send to our support team
  • Reviews or feedback you voluntarily submit
  • Survey responses (if you choose to participate)

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data only for the following purposes and legal bases:

  • Order fulfilment (Contract): Processing payments, transmitting order details to our print partner (Printful), generating shipping labels, and keeping you updated on order status.
  • Account management (Contract): Creating and maintaining your WEARIT account, enabling you to view order history and saved designs.
  • Email marketing (Consent / Legitimate interest): Sending promotional emails, new product announcements, and personalised offers. You can opt out at any time via the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or by emailing privacy@wearit.com. Note: transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates) cannot be opted out of while an order is active.
  • Analytics and site improvement (Legitimate interest): Understanding how visitors use our site so we can improve product discovery, checkout flow, and the design editor.
  • Fraud prevention and security (Legitimate interest): Detecting and preventing fraudulent transactions, chargebacks, and abuse of our AI design tools.
  • Legal compliance (Legal obligation): Retaining transaction records as required by tax and accounting law, and complying with court orders or regulatory requests.
  • Customer support (Legitimate interest): Responding to your enquiries and resolving disputes.

4. Third Parties We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following partners who are contractually bound to protect it:

Stripe — Payment Processing

Stripe processes all payment transactions. When you check out, your payment details are submitted directly to Stripe's secure servers. We share your name, email, and billing address with Stripe for fraud detection. Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant. Stripe Privacy Policy →

Printful — Print Fulfilment

Printful receives your shipping address, order details, and your design files to print and ship your products. Printful acts as a data processor under our instructions. Printful Privacy Policy →

Google Analytics — Site Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand site traffic and user behaviour. Data is anonymised (IP addresses are truncated). You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google Privacy Policy →

Anthropic — AI Design Assistant

When you use our AI-powered design assistant, text prompts are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. Anthropic may use API inputs to improve safety and model reliability in accordance with their API usage policies. We do not send personally identifiable information (such as your name or email) to Anthropic. Anthropic Privacy Policy →

Shipping Carriers

Your name, address, and (if provided) phone number are shared with shipping carriers (e.g., USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, Royal Mail) as required to deliver your order.

Legal Disclosures

We may disclose your data if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of WEARIT, our users, or the public.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our website. Here is what we use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the site to function (e.g., session authentication, shopping cart). Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics cookies to track page views, session duration, and user flow. These are anonymised and can be blocked via your browser or the Google opt-out tool.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings (e.g., preferred currency, editor tool preferences).
  • Marketing cookies: If you click on our ads on Google or Meta platforms, those platforms may set cookies to measure ad performance. We do not control these third-party cookies.

You can control cookie behaviour through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality. Our cookie banner allows you to manage non-essential cookies on your first visit.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
  • Order records: Retained for 7 years to comply with tax and accounting regulations.
  • Design uploads: Retained for 12 months after your last order to allow reprints. You can request deletion at any time.
  • Analytics data: Aggregated, anonymised analytics data is retained for up to 26 months (Google Analytics default).
  • Support communications: Retained for 3 years after your last interaction.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights over your personal data:

GDPR Rights (EU/UK residents)

  • Right of access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON or CSV).
  • Right to restrict processing: Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw marketing consent at any time without affecting previous processing.

CCPA Rights (California residents)

  • Right to know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to delete: Request deletion of personal information collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale: We do not sell your personal information. No action required.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@wearit.com. We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive data at rest
  • Access controls — only authorised personnel can access personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Stripe's PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance for all payment data

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

9. International Data Transfers

WEARIT operates from the United States. If you are in the EU or UK, your personal data will be transferred to and processed in the US. We rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for these transfers. Our third-party processors (Stripe, Google, Anthropic) are certified under applicable transfer mechanisms.

10. Children's Privacy

WEARIT is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us at privacy@wearit.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify you by email. Your continued use of WEARIT after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, contact our Privacy Team:

  • Email: privacy@wearit.com
  • Response time: within 2 business days for general enquiries; within 30 days for data subject requests

If you are in the EU and believe we have not adequately addressed your concerns, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, the CNIL in France, or the DPA in your EU member state).
