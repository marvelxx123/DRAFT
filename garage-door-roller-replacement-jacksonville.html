Roller Replacement in Jacksonville

The #1 cause of a
noisy garage door.

Garage door rollers are the small wheels that guide the door along its tracks as it opens and closes. Most doors have 10–12 rollers — steel or nylon — that take thousands of cycles over their lifetime. When they wear out, you'll hear grinding, squeaking, or feel the door shaking as it moves.

In Jacksonville, humidity and salt air accelerate roller wear — especially on the steel ball bearings inside. Worn rollers put extra load on the opener motor and tracks, which can lead to more expensive repairs down the line.

We upgrade to sealed nylon rollers that run quietly and last longer than stock steel rollers. Most roller replacements are done in under an hour. Call today.
