Spring Repair in Jacksonville

The #1 reason
garage doors fail.

A broken garage door spring is the most common repair call we get across Jacksonville. Springs are under massive tension — they do the heavy lifting every single time you open and close your door. Most springs last 7–10 years or about 10,000 cycles before they snap.

When a torsion spring breaks, you'll hear a loud bang and the door will be nearly impossible to lift manually. When an extension spring goes, the door may drop unevenly or the cable may spool off. Either way, don't try to force it — you can damage the opener, the cables, or the door itself.

We carry torsion and extension springs for every standard door size on the truck. Most spring replacements are done in 45–60 minutes. One call and it's handled.

What We Fix

Every spring problem, solved.

Parts on the truck for every standard door size in Jacksonville.

01
Torsion Spring Replacement

The single horizontal spring above the door. Most common in Jacksonville homes. We replace with properly rated springs for your door's weight — both springs at the same time to prevent uneven wear.

02
Extension Spring Replacement

The two springs that run along the tracks on each side. We replace them in pairs and inspect the safety cables that prevent a snapped spring from becoming a projectile.

03
Spring Cable Repair

When a spring breaks, cables often come off the drum or fray. We inspect and replace cables as part of any spring job — same visit, no extra call.

04
Spring Tension Adjustment

Door heavy to open, or slamming down? Spring tension may be off. We balance the door to the correct counterweight so the opener isn't overworked.

05
Commercial Spring Service

Commercial doors take heavier springs. We service commercial-grade torsion springs for garages, shops, and small commercial bays across Jacksonville.

06
Full Door Tune-Up

While we're replacing the spring, we inspect rollers, hinges, cables, and opener — catch anything else before it becomes a problem. Recommended every 2 years.
